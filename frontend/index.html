How to Play Pitchle

👀
1. Watch the 3D Pitch

Watch the ball from the batter's perspective at home plate. Observe the movement, velocity, and break towards the strike zone.

⚾
2. Step 1: Guess the Pitch Type

Select the pitch type (e.g. Fastball, Slider, Curveball). Submitting reveals the actual pitch type along with exact velocity and spin rate clues!

👤
3. Step 2: Guess the Mystery MLB Pitcher

Search and guess active MLB pitchers. Each guess evaluates 7 Statcast categories with directional feedback:

Green = Exact Match Yellow = Close Match Gray = Miss

• Team / Division: Yellow = Same Division or League (AL/NL)

• Age: Yellow = Within ±2 years. Direction: ▲ (Target is older), ▼ (Target is younger)

• Throws: Green = Exact (R or L)

• K% (Strikeout Rate): Green = Within ±1.5%, Yellow = ±4.0%. ▲/▼ Direction

• BB% (Walk Rate): Green = Within ±1.0%, Yellow = ±2.5%. ▲/▼ Direction

• Whiff% (Swing & Miss): Green = Within ±1.5%, Yellow = ±4.0%. ▲/▼ Direction

💡
4. Milestone Hints

Unlock extra clues as you keep guessing: Pitch Mix (Arsenal) unlocks at 3 guesses; Pitcher Role & Career Teams unlock at 5 guesses.

🏆
5. Winning the Game

Use all your pitch and player category clues to identify the pitcher. Guess the exact player to WIN!
